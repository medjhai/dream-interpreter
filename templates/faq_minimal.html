❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is dream interpretation? +

Dream interpretation is the process of analyzing the symbolic meaning of dreams. Our AI uses psychological principles and symbolism to provide insights into your subconscious thoughts, emotions, and experiences reflected in your dreams.

How accurate are the interpretations? +

Our interpretations are based on established psychological theories and common dream symbolism. While they provide valuable insights, remember that dream meaning is highly personal. Use our interpretations as a starting point for your own reflection.

Can I save my dream interpretations? +

Yes! When you create an account and log in, all your dreams and interpretations are automatically saved. You can review them anytime in your profile or dashboard.

What are the different interpretation styles? +

Psychological: Based on modern psychology and Jungian analysis
Spiritual: Focuses on spiritual and mystical meanings
Neutral: A balanced approach combining multiple perspectives

Is my dream data private and secure? +

Absolutely. Your dreams are personal and private. We use secure encryption to protect your data, and we never share your dream content with third parties. Only you have access to your dream journal.

Can I use voice recording for my dreams? +

Yes! You can record your dreams using voice input. Simply click the microphone button and speak naturally. The system will convert your speech to text and then provide an interpretation.

What if I don't remember my whole dream? +

That's perfectly normal! Write down whatever fragments you remember - emotions, colors, people, or situations. Even partial dreams can provide meaningful insights into your subconscious mind.
